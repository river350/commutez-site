What it does

Door-to-door, not stop-to-stop

Trips are planned from your door with real walking time, transfers and the best stop to start from.

Realtime departures

Delays and service alerts move the countdown. When you only make it by running, the trip is shown greyed out, never as the target.

Widgets & Live Activities

Home and lock screen widgets, the Dynamic Island, and an optional countdown badge on the app icon.

Built around your workday

Per-day schedules, flexitime, and 1–3 week rotations. Alerts point at the trip that gets you there on time.

Apple Watch & Mac

The same countdown on your wrist and in your menu bar. Set it up once on iPhone, the rest follows via iCloud.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Location is off unless you turn it on. Read the privacy policy.
