Privacy

No account. No servers.

Everything runs on your phone and asks the open transit and weather services directly. There is no Commutez server in between, and we don’t collect or store anything about you.

You choose how much to share. Start with just a nearby station and your own walking time. Turn on location and it gets sharper: it plans from where you stand and switches between home and work by itself.
