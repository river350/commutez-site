Privacy

No account. No servers.

Everything runs on your phone and asks the open transit and weather services directly. There is no Commutez server in between. Your position goes, rounded, only to the transit, map and weather services the app asks, and nothing reaches us unless you send feedback.Everything runs on your phone, straight against open transit and weather data. No Commutez server, and nothing reaches us unless you send feedback. Your location is only used for your commute.

Location is used for your commute: to plan from where you stand, know when you’re on your way, and switch between home and work by itself. Your addresses and settings stay on your phone and watch. Rather not? Turn location off and it works from an address or station you pick and your own walking time.
