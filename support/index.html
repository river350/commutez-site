Get in touch

The fastest way: in the app, open Settings › 04 APP › Send feedback. You can attach a screenshot and choose to include trip details, and you see everything that is sent before it goes. Replies show up in the app under Your reports.

Or email support@commutez.app.

Getting started

What happens the first time I open the app?

Three short steps: Where do you live? (you can use your current location), Where do you work? and When do you work?, where you also choose whether public holidays count. Then you see tomorrow’s trip and a setup check. Tap ✕ at any time to skip it and set things up yourself in Settings.

What is the setup check?

One screen that checks the app has the permissions it needs to work at its best. Use recommended sets it up for you, Open iOS Settings takes you to what only iOS can change, and Not now leaves everything as it is.

What does the app use my location for?

For your commute: to plan from where you stand, to switch between home and work by itself, and to know when you’re on your way. For that to work with the app closed, choose “Always” and keep Precise Location on. Under Settings › 02 LIVE & ON BOARD › Background location you choose how much: Live window (the default), Always, or Off. Without location it works from your schedule, an address or station you pick, and your own walking time. What is sent where is in the privacy policy.

Do I have to share my home address?

No. You can pick a station instead of your address and set your walking time yourself.

Day to day

How does it know if I’m going to work or going home?

It follows your schedule, and you can always flip the HOME / WORK switch at the top of the app. With location on and “Always”, it also switches by itself.

I have a day off. How do I stop the countdown?

Under your work hours in Settings, tap Days off and choose Today, Tomorrow or Until… a date. Those days have no commute: no alarm, alerts or Live Activity. The countdown says DAY OFF TODAY · TAP TO UNDO, and a tap brings the day back.

Why did the alerts stop while I was away?

When you’re away from home, alerts, the icon badge, the tap on your wrist and new Live Activities take a break by themselves. They come back on their own when you return. A trip you start yourself still works as usual.

Which notifications can I get?

Each trip has its own switches in Settings. The ones you may see:

  • Time to go / Workday’s over: when it’s time to leave. Delays are taken into account, and on the way home it also names the next departure.
  • Plans changed: when you need to leave earlier than planned.
  • Tomorrow: replacement bus for line …: a quiet heads-up the evening before, when your morning train is replaced by a bus.
  • Get off at …: a buzz before the stop where you get off, 1 minute before by default. Change it to 1–5 minutes under Stop buzz in Settings › 02 LIVE & ON BOARD.
The Live Activity didn’t start by itself

iOS only lets the app start a Live Activity while it is open. When it’s getting close to time to leave, open the app or tap one of its widgets, and the countdown moves to the lock screen and the Dynamic Island. From then on it keeps counting with the app closed until you arrive. Make sure Live Activities are allowed under iOS Settings › Commutez.

What happens when I tap a widget?

Tapping a widget or the Live Activity opens the app on the countdown. A weather tile opens the weather. In the Smart Stack, Commutez asks to move up while you’re on your way.

The widget doesn’t update every minute

The countdown keeps ticking on its own, but iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Open the app for the very latest.

Some departures are greyed out

Those are departures you can’t make in time, and cancelled ones. They stay in the list, but the countdown always aims for a trip you can make.

When does it suggest an e-scooter?

If you turn on E-scooter in Settings › 01 THE JOURNEY, the app can suggest a scooter for the walk when that gets you there sooner. Turn on Scooter only when dry and it skips the scooter in bad weather.

The lock screen widget is black and white

That’s iOS: lock screen widgets are always shown in a single tint. It’s not a bug.

Devices and coverage

How do I set it up on Apple Watch?

Set up Commutez on your iPhone first. The watch then gets what it needs and works on its own, with the same trip as your phone.

Which countries and cities are supported?

Public transport in Norway, Sweden, Finland, Estonia and Greater London. Car, bike and walking commutes work everywhere in Europe. See the full list.

How is my data handled?

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your addresses and settings stay on your phone and watch. See the privacy policy.
